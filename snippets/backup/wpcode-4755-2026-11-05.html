Hardware, irrigation, media and the paperwork.

You buy the parts and the information needed to install them properly. Wall fixings are not included, because they depend on your substrate.

07 — Straight answers

Trade questions.

Can we buy the system and install it ourselves?+

Yes. We supply Channel Pro® and Living Louvres® hardware, irrigation kits and substrate to landscapers, contractors and designers running their own installations, with setting-out drawings and remote technical support through the build.

What support do you give during the build?+

Setting-out and fixing drawings before you start, weight loadings and O&M documentation for the project file, and a technical contact on the phone while the system goes up.

Do you deliver across the UK?+

Yes, we ship UK-wide. Delivery terms and lead times are confirmed with the quotation.

Which system suits my wall?+

Channel Pro® for continuous coverage on full elevations, atria and long runs. Living Louvres® for irregular surfaces, screens and feature elevations. Living Louvres® are not used on walls under 1200 mm high.

Do you supply plants?+

We can. The plant bundle is priced at 36 plants per m², or you can source your own to our schedule. Planting on site is the standard method; pre-growing is available on request.

Is there trade pricing?+

Yes. Trade enquiries are welcome. Trade applies to a wall of 50 m² or more, or a minimum annual purchase commitment agreed at onboarding, with rates set by volume.
