Channel Pro® · Living Louvres®

Two living wall systems, every scale of façade.

Engineered in lightweight aluminium, irrigated by zone and planted for the microclimate. Specify them with us or buy them from us — we supply the trade as an independent living wall system supplier.

Request specificationsCompare the systems

01 — Channel Pro®

Continuous coverage for full elevations.

Six folded aluminium channels stack per square metre, hung on galvanised rails at 750mm centres and anchored back to the façade or an aluminium support structure. Channels are filled with engineered substrate and planted on site from 9 to 13cm pots, then trimmed out on all four sides to conceal the riser, water supplies, valves and guttering. Designed for full-height elevations, atria and long courtyard runs where the wall has to read as one continuous plane.

02 — Living Louvres®

Planted louvres that shade, screen and green.

Louvres stand off the building line and are planted on the front face, with the back face carrying the fixings, while the louvres screen glazing and break up hard elevations. They suit surfaces that cannot take a full wall build-up, including curved and irregular structures. Orientation and spacing are set before fixing, so the run can be angled or opened up without cutting anything; once fixed the louvres are static and stable.

Irrigation and site requirements

Drip line

Aster 16mm line, pressure regulated, anti-syphon and self cleaning throughout, designed to last over 15 years.

Controllers

Claber Duplo battery powered tap timer for one to two zones with no mains power required, a Rain Bird TM2 WiFi controller for two to twelve zones, or a Heron WiFi remote controller for twelve zones and above.

Valves

Rain Bird DV/DVF series (075-DV) 24V solenoid valves.

Optional equipment

Water tank, submersible pump, Rain Bird flow sensor, Dosatron feeding unit, rainwater collection system, mirrors, lighting and other bespoke features.

Plant room (recommended for large scale)

Frost free location with a mains water feed at 2.5 bar, 240V 13 amp sockets and WiFi for remote monitoring and alerts. Recommended for outdoor large scale installations that need a water tank and pump.

05 — Under the planting

The parts you never see are the parts that keep it alive.

—

Irrigation & control

Zoned drip lines sized to the wall, pressure regulated and commissioned on site. Sensor-led control and remote monitoring are available so watering responds to conditions rather than a fixed timer.

—

Substrate & drainage

Substrate depth and composition are matched to the planting palette, with drainage and overflow detailed into the build-up so water leaves the system rather than the building.

—

Fire & compliance

Both systems use a fire resistant build-up, and we provide weight loadings, fixing details and product data for building control and insurer review.

—

Circularity

Aluminium frames and demountable components can be removed and reused at end of life, which supports the circular ambitions in our own vision as well as your ESG reporting.
