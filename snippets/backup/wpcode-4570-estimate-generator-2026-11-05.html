01 — Choose a system

Two IP-protected aluminium systems, made in the UK.

Channel Pro®

£264 + VAT / m²

A modular aluminium channel design creating a dense, visually rich planted surface. Each square metre is built from six aluminium channels stacked vertically, carrying 36–42 plants. Fully planted and irrigated it weighs roughly 60–65 kg/m², which suits façades and large-scale architectural applications.

  • —36–42 plants/m²
  • —6 channels/m²
  • —60–65 kg/m² planted
  • —Powder-coatable
  • —Any wall height
  • —Aluminium ‘V’ channels, 2400 mm

Living Louvres®

£220 + VAT / m²

Vertical aluminium louvres with a specialist growing substrate, selected plants and integrated drip irrigation. Each louvre can be made to bespoke dimensions and hangs directly from the façade or a support structure. Non-combustible aluminium suits façades and interiors with strict fire-safety requirements.

  • —Non-combustible
  • —300 mm wide on 400 mm pitch
  • —100 mm gaps
  • —Standard 2400 / 1200 mm heights
  • —Powder-coatable
  • —Fire-safe indoors
