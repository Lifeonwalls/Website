We build living wall systems that stay alive after the photographs are taken.

03 — Our systems

Two systems, every scale of façade.

Living Louvres® planted louvre system

Planted aluminium louvres that stand off the building line to break up hard elevations and green surfaces that cannot take a full wall build-up, including curved and irregular structures. The louvres can be powder coated to a selected RAL colour, so the frame and the planting read together along the façade. Each louvre is planted on one face, with the back face carrying the fixings, and is set to its angle before fixing; once fixed the louvres are static.

07 — Straight answers

Living wall questions we are asked most.

Full FAQ
How much does a living wall cost in London?+

The aluminium system starts at £220 + VAT per square metre for Living Louvres® and £264 + VAT per square metre for Channel Pro®. Add roughly £27.50/m² for the drip irrigation kit, £25.30/m² for substrate and £198/m² for planting at 36 plants per square metre, then installation at one day per 5 m² within the M25. Our estimate generator prices this instantly, and we confirm the figure after a site inspection.

How often does a living wall need maintenance?+

Interior walls are visited monthly, exterior walls four to eight times a year depending on species and exposure. Each living wall maintenance visit covers irrigation checks, feeding, pruning, and replacement of any failed plants so density stays even.

Can you supply the system if we install it ourselves?+

Yes. As a living wall system supplier we sell Channel Pro® and Living Louvres® hardware, irrigation kits and substrate to landscapers and main contractors, with setting-out drawings and remote technical support through the build.

How long does installation take?+

A domestic courtyard wall is usually installed in two to four days. Larger commercial living wall installations run one to three weeks on site, programmed around scaffold, façade and M&E sequencing.

Which areas of the UK do you cover?+

We are based in Tooting, south west London, and install across Greater London and the M25 weekly, with delivered projects in Bristol, the Home Counties and internationally. System supply ships UK-wide.
