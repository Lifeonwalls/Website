## Project name

AI Payables Tracker & Telegram Reporting Assistant

## Associated with

Life on Walls (Green Lease Ltd)

## Description (about 1,300 of 2,000 characters)

Built and launched a live payables system for Life on Walls, a London company that designs, installs and maintains living walls. It gives management a 30-day view of every outgoing payment and replaces 3–4 hours of manual work each week.

The challenge
Supplier invoices arrived through personal inboxes, WhatsApp and paper receipts. Purchases were paid by company card, personal card and bank transfer, often on site. Most suppliers were on 30-day terms and the winter slowdown was coming, but there was no single view of what was due and when.

What I built
• One accounts inbox as the front door for every invoice and receipt
• An AI agent that reads each invoice and extracts the supplier, amount, VAT, job tag and due date
• A live ledger linked to QuickBooks Projects for job costing
• A 30-day dashboard that combines supplier bills with salaries, rent and card repayments, flagged red, amber or green
• A Telegram AI assistant that sends regular updates and answers questions on demand, like "what do we owe on this project?"

Results
• Live and in daily use
• 3–4 hours of manual work saved every week
• Management kept informed in real time, with custom reports in seconds from their phone
• Supplier payments planned ahead of terms through the seasonal slowdown
